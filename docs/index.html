Grid2Groove listening examples

Twelve bars from twelve GMD test performances. Each shows the expressive drum grid (EDG) our model is conditioned on, the recording, our model's output with three of its ablations (a 500 Hz grid, the pre-snap latent, and the 6-layer regressor), and TRIA's prompts next to its outputs: released, fine-tuned on GMD for 80,000 iterations, and the same fine-tune continued to 160,000.

EDG plot: for each instrument, the state velocity at every frame (shaded) and the onsets (stems as high as their velocity, coloured and shaped by articulation id; hover for details). Outputs are gain-matched to the recording's loudness without clipping, so some regression clips play quieter; prompts play as TRIA received them. One sample per bar, as evaluated.
